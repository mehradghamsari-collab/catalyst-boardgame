Mission

Curiosity, engineered into play.

Catalyst creates boardgames inspired by real-world systems, science, innovation, sustainability, and strategic decision-making. Each title starts with a true process, then turns that process into tense, elegant gameplay.

Our goal is simple: make players feel the thrill of discovery at the table, while building worlds that are credible enough for educators and exciting enough for serious gamers.

Science

Mechanics grounded in chemistry, biology, textiles, materials, and systems thinking.

Strategy

Meaningful tradeoffs, player tension, planning arcs, and adaptive decision loops.

Sustainability

Environmental pressure becomes a playable system, not a decorative theme.

Innovation

Novel themes and mechanisms built for players, classrooms, and partners.

Discovery

Every session gives players a new model for understanding the world.

Education

Learning appears through play: rules, outcomes, and memorable failures.
