About the Studio

Mehrad
& Anita

We are Catalyst Board Game Design, founded in Oxford by a chemist and a statistician who believe the world's most fascinating science belongs on the table.

Mehrad synthesises the chemistry into mechanics. Anita optimises the systems and ensures every game is as playable as it is scientifically rigorous. We design for the curious adult — and for the student who hasn't yet discovered that science can be thrilling.

"A great game is a working model of something true — a system that teaches by letting you fail, adapt, and discover."
Mehrad Niasari
Game Designer · Chemist
Oxford University · Chemistry & Materials

Mehrad's organic chemistry and sustainable materials background drives every mechanic — from authentic distillation in Jabir to real polymer science in Synthelyst. He also works in bioplastics R&D, giving Synthelyst its commercial and scientific authenticity.

Anita Mansouri
Game Designer · Statistician
Statistician in pharmaceutical industry

Anita brings statistical precision to game design — stress-testing every mechanic, optimising balance across player counts, and ensuring that Catalyst games are elegantly playable, deeply replayable, and genuinely competitive.

Our Design Philosophy

Games where you win by understanding — not just by optimising.

Every Catalyst title rests on one conviction: the deepest fun comes from the moment a system clicks in your mind. When you realise why a rule exists because the chemistry demands it — that rule becomes knowledge.

We also believe our games belong in classrooms. Science motivation disguised as an excellent evening of strategy.
