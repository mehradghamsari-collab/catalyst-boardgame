The Studio

Games that transform curiosity into strategy.

Catalyst Board Games is an Oxford science game studio founded by a chemist and a statistician. We design for curious adults, board game publishers, and students who haven't yet discovered that science can be thrilling.

Mehrad turns real chemistry into elegant game mechanics. Anita stress-tests every system with statistical precision. Every game must be both scientifically accurate and genuinely fun.
