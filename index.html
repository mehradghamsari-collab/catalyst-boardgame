About the Studio

Mehrad
& Anita

We are Catalyst Board Game Design — an Oxford science board game studio founded by a chemist and a statistician.

Mehrad turns real chemistry and science into elegant game mechanics. Anita stress-tests and balances every system. Together we design for curious adults, publishers, and students who haven't yet discovered that science can be thrilling.

"A great game is a working model of something true — a system that teaches by letting you fail, adapt, and discover."
