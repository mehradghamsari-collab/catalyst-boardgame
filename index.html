About the Studio

MEHRAD
& ANITA

Two Oxford scientists. A shared belief that the world's most fascinating chemistry belongs on the table.

We are Catalyst Board Game Design, founded in Oxford by Mehrad Niasari — chemist and researcher at Oxford University — and Anita Mansouri — statistician in the pharmaceutical industry.

Mehrad synthesises the chemistry into mechanics. Anita optimises the systems, stress-tests every interaction, and ensures every game is as playable as it is scientifically rigorous. Together we've built three titles at the intersection of science, strategy, and story.

We also believe these games belong in classrooms — gateways to show students that chemistry is one of the most creative, dramatic, and surprising disciplines that exists.

"A great game is a working model of something true — a system that teaches by letting you fail, adapt, and discover."
Mehrad Niasari
Game Designer · Chemist
Oxford University · Chemistry & Materials

Mehrad is the scientific engine of Catalyst. His organic chemistry and sustainable materials background drives every mechanic — from authentic distillation in Jabir to real polymer science in Synthelyst.

Anita Mansouri
Game Designer · Statistician
Statistician in pharmaceutical industry

Anita brings statistical precision to game design — stress-testing every mechanic, optimising balance across player counts, and ensuring that Catalyst games are elegantly playable and deeply replayable.

Our Design Philosophy

Games where you win by understanding — not just by optimising.

Every Catalyst title rests on one conviction: the deepest fun comes from the moment a system clicks in your mind. When you realise why a rule exists because the chemistry demands it — that rule becomes knowledge. That knowledge is the reward.

We also believe our games belong in schools. Science motivation disguised as an excellent evening of strategy.
