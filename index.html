Synthelyst

A Game of Biomaterial Innovation · Mid-Heavy Eurogame

2–5 Players60–90 minAge 14+Mid-Heavy

You are CEO of a bioplastics firm in 2035. Build sustainable supply chains, manage clean energy networks, and prevent ecological collapse — while outmanoeuvring rivals for market dominance. Every mechanic is grounded in real bioplastics science.

  • 5 real bioplastic feedstocks — corn, sugarcane, potato, milk, algae
  • Cooperative-competitive tension — shared Earth Health loss condition
  • Asymmetric company boards — five distinct strategic identities
  • ESG & sustainability education licensing potential
  • Prototype complete — ready to play today

WashWheel

The Fabric Science Game · Medium Weight

2–4 Players90–120 minAge 14+Medium

Run a professional textile laboratory. The rotating Wash Wheel drives a live resource market. Match detergent chemistry to each garment's real fibre profile — or risk damaging valuable pieces and losing critical points.

  • Rotating Wash Wheel board — a circular card market with 12 slots
  • Real textile chemistry — pH, temperature, enzyme science
  • 4 specialist lab roles with unique strategic approaches
  • Brand partnership potential — laundry, fashion, sustainability sectors
  • Sustainability scoring rewards eco-friendly decisions

The Studio

Mehrad & Anita.

Games that transform curiosity into strategy.

Catalyst Board Games is an Oxford science game studio founded by a chemist and a statistician. We design for curious adults, board game publishers, and students who haven't yet discovered that science can be thrilling.

Mehrad turns real chemistry and scientific research into elegant mechanics. Anita stress-tests every system with statistical rigour. Every game must be both scientifically accurate and genuinely compelling to play.
