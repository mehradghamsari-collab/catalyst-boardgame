Game 001 · Heavy Eurogame

Jabir:
The Perfumer

Distil nature into legendary fragrances.

A heavy eurogame of real perfumery chemistry. Harvest rare botanicals from six global regions, transform them through authentic extraction methods, age to peak potency, and compose legendary fragrances to claim the title of Grand Alchemist.

  • Harvest 41 unique botanical extracts globally
  • Four authentic extraction methods
  • Maturation mechanics — patience is power
  • Compose via the fragrance pyramid
  • Synthesise rare aromatic molecules

Game 002 · Mid-Heavy Eurogame

Synthe-
lyst

Transform renewable feedstocks into the materials of tomorrow.

A cooperative-competitive eco-strategy game set in 2035. You are CEO of a bioplastics company — harvest sustainable feedstocks, build energy systems, polymerise bioplastics, resolve global crises, and keep Earth Health above zero.

  • 5 feedstocks: corn, milk, algae, potato, sugarcane
  • Build solar, wind, hydro, and fossil energy networks
  • Resolve global crisis cards collaboratively
  • Engine-building Catalyst Deck mechanics
  • Cooperative survival + individual victory

About the Studio

Mehrad
& Anita

We are Catalyst Board Game Design, founded in Oxford by a chemist and a statistician who believe the world's most fascinating science belongs on the table.

Mehrad synthesises the chemistry into mechanics. Anita optimises the systems and ensures every game is as playable as it is scientifically rigorous.

"A great game is a working model of something true — a system that teaches by letting you fail, adapt, and discover."
Mehrad Niasari
Game Designer · Chemist
Oxford University · Chemistry & Materials

Organic chemistry and bioplastics R&D background drives every mechanic — from distillation in Jabir to polymer science in Synthelyst.

Anita Mansouri
Game Designer · Statistician
Statistician in pharmaceutical industry

Brings statistical precision to game design — stress-testing mechanics, optimising balance, ensuring Catalyst games are elegant and genuinely competitive.

Design Philosophy

Games where you win by understanding — not just by optimising.

Every Catalyst title rests on one conviction: the deepest fun comes from the moment a system clicks in your mind. When you realise a rule exists because the chemistry demands it — that rule becomes knowledge.

We also believe our games belong in classrooms. Science motivation disguised as an excellent evening of strategy.
