The Perfumer

A Game of Perfumery Chemistry

2–6 Players 90–150 min Heavy Age 14+

Step into the world of a master perfumer. Harvest aromatic botanicals across 6 global regions, extract essences using 4 real chemical methods, and compose fragrances that score across the fragrance pyramid. The first eurogame built on authentic perfumery chemistry.

  • ⚗
    41 real botanical extracts from 6 global harvesting regions
  • 🔬
    4 extraction methods — steam, solvent, cold press, enfleurage
  • 🏆
    Fragrance pyramid scoring — top, heart, base notes
  • 📚
    Educational IP — real science embedded in every mechanic

About the Studio

Mehrad
& Anita

We are Catalyst Board Game Design — an Oxford science board game studio founded by a chemist and a statistician. We design for curious adults, board game publishers, and students who haven't yet discovered that science can be thrilling.

Mehrad turns real chemistry into elegant game mechanics. Anita stress-tests and balances every system with statistical precision. Every game we release must be both scientifically accurate and genuinely fun to play.

Jabir: The Perfumer
Not at all. Jabir teaches everything through play. The chemistry is embedded in the mechanics so naturally that players absorb it without realising. By your second game you'll be thinking like a real perfumer.
90–150 minutes across 5 rounds. Experienced players finish closer to 90 minutes. First-time players should allow 150 minutes. A simplified first-play variant reduces this to around 75 minutes.

Synthelyst
All players must work together to keep Earth Health above zero — if it hits zero, everyone loses immediately. But within that shared survival condition, players compete individually for Victory Points. This tension is the heart of the game.
Yes. Mehrad works professionally in bioplastics R&D. Every feedstock, energy system, and polymerisation mechanic reflects real industrial processes — trade-offs that players in the industry will immediately recognise.
Earth Health starts at 25 and decreases with every unresolved Crisis and unsustainable energy choice. If it reaches zero, all players lose regardless of VP scores — creating genuine moral and strategic tension throughout.
Absolutely. Synthelyst teaches why plastic pollution is hard to solve, how collective action problems work, and what bioplastics actually are. It is being considered for use in university sustainability modules and corporate ESG training.
Each company has a unique Core ability (usable once per round) and a Big Bet ability (usable once per game). These create distinct playstyles — Brazil's TerraCycle benefits from sugarcane speed, while Germany's Origin Polymer excels at energy efficiency.
WashWheel
The Wash Wheel is the central rotating market — a circular board with 12 slots across 4 zones. Resources move around the wheel each round. Timing your acquisitions to the wheel's rotation is as important as understanding the textile chemistry.
No prior knowledge required. WashWheel teaches real textile chemistry through play — you'll learn why wool needs acidic detergent, why silk can't go above 30°C, and why enzyme-based detergents are dangerous to delicate fibres. Each player has a science reference card at all times.
The Chemist gains formula flexibility; the Ecologist earns bonuses for sustainable choices; the Specialist masters specific fibre types; and the Innovator can acquire one card from the Wheel for free each round. Each role rewards a different strategic approach.
WashWheel has natural co-branding potential with laundry brands (Persil, Ariel, Ecover), luxury fashion houses concerned with garment longevity, and sustainable textile organisations. The sustainability message aligns with current industry communication priorities.
WashWheel is in active mechanics design. The core concept and key systems are locked. The prototype is being built and playtesting will begin soon. Publishers interested in early involvement are welcome to reach out now.
Mise en Scène
Not at all. While the Parisian fashion theme gives immediate appeal beyond traditional hobbyists, Mise en Scène is a genuine strategy game with multi-layered scoring, hidden objectives, and shared-information tension. Set collection and tableau-building fans will find deep strategic satisfaction.
Each player receives a Secret Client Card at game start — a hidden personal scoring objective (e.g. "score bonus if your outfit uses only two colour palettes"). It is kept face-down throughout play and revealed only at final scoring, adding a dramatic wildcard layer.
Adults 14+ with a focus on audiences new to strategy gaming through the fashion entry point. Ideal for publishers seeking to expand beyond traditional board game demographics, particularly among women and creative communities.
Significant. Mise en Scène could feature real fashion house cards (Chanel, Dior, Saint Laurent), creating co-branding opportunities similar to licensed sports card games. The premium Parisian aesthetic makes it a natural fit for retail in luxury boutiques and fashion week activations.
Yes — the full rulebook and sample card designs are available to publishers and interested partners on request. Email us at duo.boardgame.design@gmail.com or use the contact form above. We can also arrange a video walkthrough of the game concept.
