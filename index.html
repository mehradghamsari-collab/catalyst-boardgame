About the Studio

MEHRAD
& ANITA

Two designers. One shared conviction: the most profound scientific ideas in the world deserve to be explored through play.

We are Catalyst Board Game Design, an independent studio founded by Mehrad Niasari and Anita Mansouri. Every game we make is grounded in real science — the chemistry of fragrance, the engineering of sustainable plastics, the physics of textile care.

Our philosophy is simple: a great game is a working model of something true. When players understand why the rules exist — because enzymes denature silk proteins, because CO₂ accumulates when fossil plants run — they don't just win. They learn.

We design for the curious adult: players who want more than entertainment. Players who leave the table knowing something real — and wanting to play again immediately.

"Science and strategy are not opposites. The best decisions emerge from understanding systems — and that is exactly what games are made of."
MN
Mehrad Niasari
Co-Designer · Studio Lead

Mehrad drives the mechanical architecture and scientific research behind each game. He ensures every rule is grounded in real-world chemistry — making the science not just accurate, but playable, surprising, and genuinely illuminating.

AM
Anita Mansouri

Anita shapes the narrative and aesthetic dimension of each project — transforming abstract chemical processes into rich, inhabitable game worlds where every card tells a story and every decision feels meaningful at the table.

GAME 001 — JABIR: THE PERFUMER
JABIR:
THE PERFUMER
The Chemistry of Perfumery

A heavy strategic eurogame. Each player leads a fragrance house, travelling through 6 global growing regions — Tropical, Temperate, Boreal, Arid, Wetlands, Alpine — to harvest 94 unique botanical scents. Process raw materials through 4 authentic extraction methods (steam distillation, cold-pressing, solvent extraction, supercritical CO₂), age extracts to peak Maturity, visit 6 synthetic labs for aromatic molecules, and assemble secret Perfume Cards across Top, Heart, and Base note layers. Upgrade your alcohol grade from 70% to 99% for bonus VP. The player with the most VP after 10 rounds is crowned Grand Alchemist.

🌿   Bioplastics Engineering · Year 2035
GAME 002 — SYNTHELYST
SYNTHE-
LYST
A Competitive Eco-Strategy Game

The year is 2035. You are the CEO of a pioneering bioplastics firm racing to produce sustainable polymers from corn, milk, algae, potato, and sugarcane instead of crude oil. Harvest resources from fragile ecosystems, build energy systems (wind, solar, hydro, fossil), polymerise bioplastics, and resolve 8–20 global Crisis Cards — all while the shared Earth Health tracker ticks downward. Everyone must cooperate to stop planetary collapse. Only one player wins by having the most VP. Variable company powers, engine building, and a ticking ecological clock make every game different.

🫧   Textile Chemistry · The Fabric Science Game
GAME 003 — LATHERWORK
LATHER-
WORK
The Fabric Science Game

Each player operates a professional textile care laboratory. Garment Mission Cards arrive with real fibre compositions (silk, wool, elastane, cotton, polyester blends) and precise science-grounded care requirements. Build the perfect wash formula: the right detergent chemistry, pH range, water temperature, wash cycle, and additives. Resources flow through the rotating central Wash Wheel market. Crisis Events — hard water, enzyme shortages, colour bleeds, energy rationing — disrupt your plans. Choose one of four Lab specialisations (Chemist, Ecologist, Specialist, Innovator) and advance your Research Track for end-game bonuses.

Our Design Philosophy

Games where you win by understanding — not just by optimising.

Every Catalyst title is built on one foundational conviction: the deepest fun comes from the moment a system clicks in your mind. When you understand why your silk garment must be washed cold — because protein fibres denature above 30°C — the rule stops feeling arbitrary. It becomes knowledge. That knowledge is the game.

⚗️
Scientific Integrity

Every mechanism is anchored in real science. We research exhaustively so gameplay reflects genuine processes — and players leave more knowledgeable than when they sat down.

🌿
Emergent Systems

Stories emerge from the interaction of rules, not from scripts. Our games create genuine drama through the collision of strategy, ecology, and consequence.

🔬
Purposeful Play

Entertainment and education are not opposites. We build for curious adults who want the table to matter — and leave feeling the world is a richer place.

♻️
Thematic Depth

From the fragrance pyramid of perfumery to the polymer chains of bioplastics — our themes are not cosmetic. They are structural. Science is the mechanic.
