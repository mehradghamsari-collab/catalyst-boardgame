MISE EN
SCÈNE

A Parisian Fashion Card Game

Players2–5
Duration45–75 min
Age14+
WeightMedium
StatusConcepting

"Style is a way to say who you are without having to speak." — Rachel Zoe

In Mise en Scène, players compete as Parisian stylists building the season's most exquisite looks. Draw from a deck of beautifully curated fashion pieces, edit your selections, and build an outfit tableau that earns points through harmony, audacity, and trend awareness.

The Concept

Curate the Season's
Most Iconic Look.

Step into the world of Paris Fashion Week. The game captures the true art of styling: it is not merely about collecting pieces — it is about curating a mood. One bold Statement piece anchors your look; every other card must serve it. Draft exquisite fashion pieces, style breathtaking outfits, and outshine your rivals with harmony, bold statements, and trend awareness.

The Outfit Tableau

Up to 6 Outfit
Slots per Look

Each player builds a Mise en Scène — an outfit with slots for Outerwear, Top, Bottoms, Shoes, Hat/Accessory, and Bag. Playing a Dress fills both Top and Bottoms simultaneously.

★ Statement The hero piece — only ONE per outfit. Speaks loudly.
◆ Supporting Strong secondary piece — complements the statement.
◇ Base Foundational pieces — anchor the look. Let everything else listen.
The Cards

80 Fashion Cards.
Five Attributes Each.

Every Fashion Card has a Style Tier, Style Tag, Colour Palette, Fashion Number, and Season — all of which interact in scoring. The interplay between these attributes creates endless styling strategies.

Style Tag
Animal · Noir · Neutral · Pop · Earth · Ivory
Colour Palette
5 distinct palettes — key to Harmony scoring
Fashion Number
1–10 · matching numbers = Trend Combo bonus
Season
SS (Spring/Summer) or AW (Autumn/Winter)
Card Effect
Unique effect per card — conditional point bonuses & modifiers

How to Play

Three Steps.
Every Turn.

Each turn you Draw (3 cards from the deck or 1 from the Trend Row), Edit (keep 2, discard 1 to the shared Trend Row), and Play (place cards into your outfit tableau). The Trend Row is a shared discard visible to all — it's both a resource and a window into rival strategies.

🎴
Card Drafting

Draw from the deck or pick from the Trend Row. Taking from the Row signals your strategy — others will notice.

🏗️
Tableau Building

Build your Mise en Scène across 6 outfit slots. Every placement is a commitment — choose wisely.

🎨
Set Collection

Collect cards sharing palettes, numbers, or style tags to unlock powerful harmony bonuses.

🌟
Pattern Matching

Cards with the same Fashion Number are compatible. Build toward Trend Combos for the highest single-chain bonus.

💅
Special Actions

Muse Card: swap a card ignoring slot rules. Démodé Card: cancel a rival's top harmony bonus. Each used once.

📋
Hidden Objectives

Secret Client Cards give each player a personal scoring goal — revealed only at end game. Read your client. Style for them.

Scoring System

Three Layers.
Cumulative Points.

Scoring unfolds in sequence — Outfit Completion, then Style Harmony, then the Parisian Rules. Fashion Brief bonuses modify everything. Secret Client Cards add a hidden wildcard at the end.

Layer 1
Outfit Completion

Base points for how many outfit slots you have filled. A complete outfit with all 6 pieces scores a bonus. Every slot counts — even the accessory.

Layer 2
Style Harmony Bonuses

Monochrome Moment · Trend Combo · Effortless Chic · Season Story · Controlled Chaos. Each rewards specific card combinations across your tableau. These are the game's deepest strategic layer.

Layer 3
The Parisian Rules

Bonus points for number of cards, seasons represented, and the classic Parisian principle of one bold statement in an otherwise refined look.

Variable
Fashion Brief + Secret Client

Three Fashion Brief Cards visible to all players modify scoring for the entire game. At end game, each player reveals their Secret Client — a hidden personal objective that can swing the final score dramatically.

Why Mise en Scène?

Unique Value

👗
Fashion Industry Partnerships

Natural co-branding potential with fashion houses, luxury brands, and editorial publications. The Parisian aesthetic opens doors far beyond board game audiences.

👩‍🎤
Expanding Audience

Fashion and styling themes attract players who would never typically engage with strategy games — particularly women and creative audiences new to the hobby.

♟️
Genuine Depth

Multi-layered scoring, hidden objectives, and shared information create deep strategic decisions. Elegant enough to teach in minutes — rich enough for 100 plays.

🏆
No Competition

No comparable fashion-themed eurogame exists. Mise en Scène combines premium art direction with genuine mechanical depth in a completely novel category.
