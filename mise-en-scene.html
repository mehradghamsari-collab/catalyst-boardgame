How It Plays

Deep strategy. Real science.

Every mechanic is grounded in authentic science. Every decision is meaningful.

👗
80 Fashion Cards
Outerwear, tops, bottoms, shoes, hats, bags, dresses — each with Style Tag, Colour Palette, Fashion Number, Season, and Card Effect.
🏗️
Tableau Building
Build your Mise en Scène across 6 outfit slots. One Statement piece only — everything else must complement it or scores are lost.
🎨
Harmony Scoring
Monochrome Moment, Trend Combo, Effortless Chic, Season Story, Controlled Chaos — five harmony bonuses reward specific card combinations.
📋
Secret Client Cards
Each player holds a hidden personal scoring objective. Revealed only at end game — creating a dramatic wildcard in the final score.
💅
Special Actions
Muse Card: swap a card ignoring slot rules. Démodé Card: cancel a rival's top harmony bonus. Each used only once per game.
🌟
Fashion Brief Cards
Three public scoring modifiers visible to all — shaping the entire game's strategy before a single card is played.

Fashion Cards

Every card is a real garment archetype — with Style Tag, Colour Palette, Fashion Number, Season, and a unique Card Effect.
