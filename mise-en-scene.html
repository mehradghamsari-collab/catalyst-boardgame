About the Game

A premium card game that brings Paris Fashion Week to the table — and opens board gaming to an entirely new audience.

Mise en Scène places players as Parisian stylists competing to curate the season's most iconic looks. Draft exquisite fashion pieces, build your outfit tableau, and outscore rivals through harmony, audacity, and trend awareness. One bold Statement piece anchors your look — everything else must serve it.

The game is designed with a dual audience in mind: strategy gamers who love set collection and tableau building will find real mechanical depth, while fashion and lifestyle audiences will be drawn in by the premium Parisian aesthetic and immediately accessible theme.

Mise en Scène represents a significant commercial opportunity for publishers seeking to expand beyond traditional board game demographics. No comparable fashion eurogame exists in the current market.

Key Strengths

What makes
Mise en Scène exceptional.

👗
80 Fashion Cards
Outerwear, tops, bottoms, shoes, accessories, and bags — each card beautifully illustrated with Parisian editorial art and carrying meaningful strategic attributes.
🎨
Multi-Layer Harmony Scoring
Players score through five distinct harmony systems — Monochrome Moment, Trend Combo, Effortless Chic, Season Story, Controlled Chaos — rewarding different strategic approaches.
🎭
Secret Client Objectives
Each player holds a hidden personal scoring objective, revealed only at final scoring. This creates a dramatic wildcard layer that keeps every game unpredictable until the final reveal.
💎
Luxury Brand IP Potential
Mise en Scène could feature real fashion house cards — Chanel, Dior, Saint Laurent — creating co-branding opportunities comparable to licensed sports card games, with retail potential in luxury boutiques worldwide.
👩‍🎤
Audience Expansion
The fashion theme is a direct gateway to demographics traditionally underrepresented in strategy gaming — particularly women and creative communities. This is a significant commercial differentiator.
🌟
Parisian Premium Aesthetic
The art direction is editorial, luxurious, and aspirational. It is designed to sit comfortably in high-end retail environments alongside fashion and lifestyle products, not only in board game specialist stores.

Fashion Cards

Each card is a real garment archetype — illustrated with editorial precision and carrying Style Tag, Palette, Fashion Number, and Season attributes.
