The Game

The world's first
perfumery eurogame.

Step into the workshop of a master perfumer. Harvest aromatic botanicals across six global regions, extract essences using four authentic chemical methods, and compose fragrances that score across the fragrance pyramid. The science is real. The competition is fierce.

41 Real Botanicals

Rose, oud, jasmine, bergamot, vetiver — every token is a real aromatic species from the world's great growing regions.

How It Plays

Deep strategy.
Real science.

Every turn, every action is grounded in authentic perfumery chemistry. No other eurogame asks you to think like a real scientist.

🌍
Harvesting
Collect 41 real botanical extracts from 6 global regions — each with unique availability windows and extraction compatibility.
⚗️
Extraction
Choose from steam distillation, solvent extraction, cold pressing, or enfleurage — each yielding different aromatic profiles and VP.
🧪
Composition
Layer top, heart, and base notes into a fragrance pyramid. Score for harmony, boldness, and rarity across 5 rounds.
🏆
Grand Alchemist
The player whose fragrance portfolio earns the most prestige becomes Grand Alchemist of the season. One winner. No ties.
🔬
Maturation Tracks
Age your essences for greater potency — but time your harvest windows or lose the opportunity entirely.
⚡
Crisis Events
Seasonal market disruptions, crop failures, and competitor interference keep every game unpredictable and tense.

Botanical Cards

Each card is a real species — scientifically named, illustrated with botanical precision, and mechanically true to its chemistry.
