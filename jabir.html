JABIR:
THE PERFUMER

The Chemistry of Perfumery

Players2–6
Duration90–150 min
Age14+
WeightHeavy

"Harvest the rarest botanicals of the world. Distil. Age. Compose. Become the Grand Alchemist — the finest nose of your generation."

Jabir is a heavy strategy game of real perfumery chemistry. Every rule has a chemical reason. Every extraction method is authentic. You will leave this game knowing more about how perfume is made than most people learn in a lifetime.

The Origin Story

WHY
JABIR?

It began in an undergraduate organic chemistry lab. Mehrad was synthesising isoamyl acetate — the molecule responsible for the smell of banana — when something clicked. He had spent years building a deep understanding of how aromatic molecules are extracted, distilled, and combined. He knew what most people never learn: that a bottle of Chanel No. 5 contains over 80 ingredients; that rose absolute requires five tonnes of petals per kilogram; that synthetic musks were discovered almost by accident.

Every time he shared these facts — at dinners, at conferences, with friends — people were captivated. "Why isn't there a game about this?" he kept thinking. A game where the chemistry isn't background flavour, but the actual engine of play.

Jabir is named after Jabir ibn Hayyan — the 8th-century Arab polymath widely considered the father of chemistry, and the inventor of the alembic still used in perfumery to this day. The game also carries a personal mission: to bring more women into the board game world, through a theme that resonates broadly and mechanics that reward knowledge over aggression.

Unique Market Position

No comparable game exists. Jabir combines heavy eurogame mechanics with scientifically accurate perfumery chemistry — creating a title that can partner with fragrance brands for marketing campaigns, be used in chemistry education, and open the hobby to entirely new audiences.

Game Mechanics

HOW TO
PLAY

1 🌍
GLOBAL HARVEST

Roll the Region Die to determine which of 6 geographic zones is active — Tropical, Temperate, Boreal, Arid, Wetlands, or Alpine. Place your 3 workers on plant categories and roll dice to harvest up to 94 unique botanical scents. Bet on the region in advance for bonus coins.

⚗️ Real science: Bulgarian rose vs. Turkish rose produce different chemical profiles
2 🔥
EXTRACTION

Choose your extraction method for each harvested botanical: Steam Distillation, Cold-Pressing, Solvent Extraction, or Supercritical CO₂. Each method produces different Potency and Maturity profiles — just as in industrial perfumery.

⚗️ Real science: CO₂ extraction preserves heat-sensitive molecules steam destroys
3 ⏳
MATURATION

Age your extracts in storage to increase their Maturity score. Pay maintenance costs or waive them — but waiving risks quality degradation. Timing your harvest-to-bottle cycle is the game's deepest strategic layer.

⚗️ Real science: Oakmoss absolute improves with aging; citrus top notes do not
4 🧪
SYNTHETIC LABS

Visit one of 6 laboratory benches to synthesise aromatic molecules — Hedione, Ambroxan, Iso E Super — that amplify your naturals. Upgrade your alcohol grade from 70% to 99% for bonus VP and superior projection.

⚗️ Real science: Iso E Super is present in nearly every major woody fragrance since 1980
5 🌸
PERFUME CREATION

Assemble your secret Perfume Cards using Top, Heart, and Base notes from the shared market. Cards range from Standard (9 VP) to Luxury (15 VP). Add a Fixative molecule for bonus VP and improved longevity scores.

⚗️ Real science: The fragrance pyramid reflects molecular volatility rates
6 🏆
FINAL SCORING

After 10 rounds, the player with the most Victory Points is crowned Grand Alchemist. VP comes from completed perfume cards, alcohol grade upgrades, synthetic molecules, and fixative bonuses.

⚗️ Real science: Projection vs. longevity — the eternal perfumer's trade-off

The Science

REAL CHEMISTRY.
REAL RULES.

Every rule in Jabir has a chemical reason. Steam distillation destroys heat-sensitive molecules — that's why cold-pressing is the only method for citrus. Supercritical CO₂ extraction preserves the full aromatic profile — and costs more. Oakmoss ages beautifully; citrus top notes fade.

The Alcohol Grade Tracker reflects real ethanol's role as a solvent: 99% pharmaceutical-grade alcohol dissolves more aromatic molecules, produces a crystal-clear mixture, and evaporates more elegantly on skin.

Players who understand the science make better decisions. But the rules teach the science — so players learn as they play.
