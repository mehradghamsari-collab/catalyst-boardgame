About the Game

The world's first eurogame built on authentic perfumery chemistry.

Jabir places players in the role of master perfumers competing across five seasons of harvest, extraction, and composition. Every mechanic is grounded in the real science of fragrance creation — from regional botany to molecular extraction chemistry.

Named after Jabir ibn Hayyan, the 8th-century Arab chemist who invented distillation, the game brings one of history's most sophisticated crafts to the modern strategy gaming table.

Jabir is not merely a game about perfume. It is a game about mastery, patience, and the chemistry of beauty — presented in a format that publishers can position for luxury retail, science education, and brand partnerships with fragrance houses worldwide.

Key Strengths

What makes
Jabir: The Perfumer exceptional.

⚗️
Real Chemistry
Every extraction method — steam distillation, solvent extraction, cold pressing, enfleurage — reflects actual industrial perfumery science. Players engage with genuine material science.
🌍
Global Botany
41 aromatic botanical species across six real growing regions — from the rose fields of Bulgaria to the oud forests of Southeast Asia. Every extract token is scientifically named.
🏆
Fragrance Pyramid
The scoring system mirrors the real fragrance pyramid: top, heart, and base notes score differently, rewarding deep knowledge and thoughtful composition strategy.
💎
Luxury IP Potential
Natural co-branding opportunity with premium fragrance houses such as Creed, Maison Margiela, or Chanel. The aesthetic and subject matter open retail markets far beyond traditional board game channels.
🎓
Education Ready
Designed for the classroom as much as the gaming table. Currently being considered for secondary school chemistry, university chemistry outreach, and museum science programming.
📦
Prototype Complete
The full prototype is ready to play today. Rulebook finalised. Final art complete. Press kit, science documentation, and design files available immediately on request.

Botanical Cards

Each card carries a real species name, extraction method, and fragrance notes — beautiful, precise, and mechanically true to its chemistry.
