JABIR:
THE PERFUMER

The Chemistry of Perfumery

Players2–6
Duration90–150 min
Age14+
WeightHeavy
Rounds5

"Harvest the rarest botanicals of the world. Distil. Age. Compose. Become the Grand Alchemist."

Jabir is a heavy strategy game of real perfumery chemistry. Every rule has a chemical reason. Every extraction method is authentic. You will leave this game knowing more about how perfume is made than most people learn in a lifetime.

The Origin Story

WHY
JABIR?

It began in an undergraduate organic chemistry lab. Mehrad was synthesising the molecule responsible for the smell of banana when something clicked. He had spent years building a deep understanding of how aromatic molecules are extracted, distilled, and combined — and every time he shared these facts, people were captivated.

"Why isn't there a game about this?" Named after Jabir ibn Hayyan — the 8th-century polymath considered the father of chemistry and inventor of the alembic still — the game carries a personal mission: to bring more people into the board game world through a theme with universal appeal.

Unique Market Position

No comparable game exists. Jabir combines heavy eurogame mechanics with scientifically accurate perfumery chemistry — creating a title that can partner with fragrance brands, support chemistry education, and open the hobby to entirely new audiences.

Game Mechanics

HOW TO
PLAY

Jabir unfolds over 5 rounds, each flowing through interconnected phases that mirror real stages of professional fragrance creation — from the field to the bottle.

🌍
Harvest

Travel through six distinct growing regions of the world. Gather rare botanical materials in a competitive, timing-driven race where scarcity is real.

🔥
Extraction

Transform your botanicals using one of four authentic processing methods. Each yields a different quality profile — choosing wisely distinguishes great perfumers from good ones.

⏳
Maturation

Allow your extracts to develop over time. Patience builds quality — but every delay carries a cost. Managing this tension is at the heart of Jabir's strategy.

🧪
Laboratory

Acquire aromatic molecules in the modern synthesis lab that amplify and elevate your naturals. Upgrade your formulation grade for greater scoring potential.

🌸
Compose

Assemble your secret Perfume Cards by layering notes across the fragrance pyramid — Top, Heart, and Base. This is where every earlier decision pays off.

🏆
Scoring

VP are tallied across all creative and strategic choices made throughout the game. The player with the most is crowned Grand Alchemist.

The Ingredient Library

41 UNIQUE
EXTRACTS

41 aromatic extracts drawn from the real lexicon of fine perfumery — sourced from six distinct growing regions, each belonging to a specific layer in the fragrance pyramid.

🌴
Tropical

Equatorial botanicals — rich, dense, and intensely aromatic. Some of perfumery's most coveted raw materials.

🌿
Temperate

The classic European garden. Home to many of the world's most recognised fragrance ingredients.

🌲
Boreal

Northern forests and cold-adapted flora. Rare, wild materials that add depth and unexpected character.

🌵
Arid

Desert blooms and heat-resistant resins. Scarce and highly valued — the rarest harvests in the game.

🌊
Wetlands

Rivers, marshes, and aquatic plant life. Unexpected materials with freshness and otherness.

⛰️
Alpine

High-altitude flowers and mountain materials. Most difficult to harvest — and most richly rewarded.

The Science

Real Chemistry.
Real Rules.

Every rule in Jabir exists because the chemistry demands it. The extraction method you choose shapes the quality and character of what you create. How you age your materials changes what they become.

Players who understand the underlying science make better decisions — but Jabir teaches that science through play. You don't need to be a chemist to win. But by the time you finish your first game, you'll think like one.
