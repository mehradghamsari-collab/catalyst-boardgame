JABIR:
THE PERFUMER

The Chemistry of Perfumery

Players2–6
Duration90–150 min
Age14+
WeightHeavy
Rounds5

"Harvest the rarest botanicals of the world. Distil. Age. Compose. Become the Grand Alchemist — the finest nose of your generation."

Jabir is a heavy strategy game of real perfumery chemistry. Every rule has a chemical reason. Every extraction method is authentic. You will leave this game knowing more about how perfume is made than most people learn in a lifetime.

The Origin Story

WHY
JABIR?

It began in an undergraduate organic chemistry lab. Mehrad was synthesising the molecule responsible for the smell of banana when something clicked. He had spent years building a deep understanding of how aromatic molecules are extracted, distilled, and combined — and every time he shared these facts, people were captivated.

"Why isn't there a game about this?" he kept thinking. A game where the chemistry isn't background flavour, but the actual engine of play. Named after Jabir ibn Hayyan — the 8th-century polymath considered the father of chemistry and inventor of the alembic still — the game also carries a personal mission: to bring more people, especially women, into the board game world through a theme with universal appeal.

Unique Market Position

No comparable game exists. Jabir combines heavy eurogame mechanics with scientifically accurate perfumery chemistry — creating a title that can partner with fragrance brands, support chemistry education, and open the hobby to entirely new audiences.

Game Mechanics

HOW TO
PLAY

Jabir unfolds over 5 rounds, each flowing through a series of interconnected phases. Every step mirrors a real stage in how professional fragrances are made — from the field to the bottle. The science drives the strategy.

🌍
HARVEST

Travel through six distinct growing regions of the world, each with its own aromatic character. Gather raw botanical materials in a competitive, timing-driven race.

🔥
EXTRACTION

Transform your botanicals into aromatic extracts using one of four authentic processing methods. Each yields a different quality profile — choosing wisely is the mark of a skilled perfumer.

⏳
MATURATION

Allow your extracts to develop over time. Patience builds quality — but every delay carries a cost. Managing this tension is at the heart of Jabir's strategy.

🧪
LABORATORY

Step into the modern synthesis lab to acquire aromatic molecules that amplify your naturals. Upgrade your formulation to unlock higher scoring potential.

🌸
COMPOSE

Assemble your secret Perfume Cards by layering notes across the fragrance pyramid. This is where every earlier decision either pays off — or falls short.

🏆
SCORING

At the close of the final round, VP are tallied across all your creative and strategic choices. The player with the most is crowned Grand Alchemist.

The Ingredient Library

41 UNIQUE
EXTRACTS

41 aromatic extracts drawn from the real lexicon of fine perfumery — sourced from six distinct growing regions, each belonging to a specific layer in the fragrance pyramid.

🌴
Tropical

Equatorial botanicals — rich, dense, and intensely aromatic. Some of perfumery's most coveted raw materials.

🌿
Temperate

The classic European garden. Home to many of the world's most recognised fragrance ingredients.

🌲
Boreal

Northern forests and cold-adapted flora. Rare, wild materials that add depth and unexpected character.

🌵
Arid

Desert blooms and heat-resistant resins. Scarce and highly valued — the rarest harvests in the game.

🌊
Wetlands

Rivers, marshes, and aquatic plant life. Unexpected materials with freshness and an otherworldly quality.

⛰️
Alpine

High-altitude flowers and mountain materials. The most difficult to harvest — and most richly rewarded.

The Science

REAL CHEMISTRY.
REAL RULES.

Every rule in Jabir exists because the chemistry demands it. The extraction method you choose shapes the quality and character of what you create. How you age your materials changes what they become.

Players who understand the underlying science make better decisions — but Jabir teaches that science through play. You don't need to be a chemist to win. But by the time you finish your first game, you'll think like one.

Unique Value

WHY
JABIR?

🌹
Brand Partnership Potential

Jabir can feature real perfume brands' signature fragrances — a natural marketing collaboration for houses like Chanel, Dior, or Jo Malone. Unique co-branding potential in the luxury fragrance market.

👩
Expanding the Audience

Perfumery is a theme with strong appeal beyond traditional board game demographics — particularly women and creative audiences who don't yet know they love strategy games.

🎓

Jabir can be used in secondary schools and universities as a chemistry teaching tool — covering organic chemistry, biochemistry, and extraction processes in a memorable format.

No comparable game exists. Jabir occupies a completely novel category: a heavy strategy eurogame built on scientifically accurate perfumery chemistry.
