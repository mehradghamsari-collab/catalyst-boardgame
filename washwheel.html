About the Game

A medium-weight strategy game where real textile chemistry meets an ingenious rotating market — and natural co-branding potential across the laundry, fashion, and sustainability sectors.

WashWheel puts players in charge of a professional textile laboratory. At the heart of the table sits the rotating Wash Wheel — a unique mechanical component that creates a dynamic, ever-shifting resource economy.

Every garment in WashWheel reflects real fabric science. Players engage with authentic textile chemistry — pH sensitivity, enzyme compatibility, temperature tolerances — in a form that feels intuitive rather than technical.

Nardshir welcomes early publishing conversations: WashWheel is at the stage where co-shaping component design, brand partnerships, and market positioning together with a publisher would create the strongest commercial outcome.

The Wash Wheel

A rotating circular card market — resources move clockwise each round, making timing your acquisition as critical as your chemistry knowledge.
