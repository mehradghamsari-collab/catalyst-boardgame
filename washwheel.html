About the Game

A medium-weight strategy game where real textile chemistry meets an ingenious rotating market.

WashWheel puts players in charge of a professional textile laboratory. At the heart of the table sits the Wash Wheel — a circular card market that rotates each turn, creating a dynamic resource economy that rewards timing and scientific knowledge in equal measure.

Every garment card in WashWheel reflects real fabric science. Players must match detergent chemistry to fibre profiles — understanding pH sensitivity, enzyme compatibility, and temperature tolerances — to achieve maximum cleaning results without damaging valuable pieces.

WashWheel is designed for publishers seeking a game with strong brand partnership potential across the laundry, fashion, and sustainability sectors, alongside accessible mechanics that make complex science feel intuitive and rewarding.

Key Strengths

What makes
WashWheel exceptional.

🫧
The Wash Wheel
The central rotating card market is WashWheel's signature component — a circular board with 12 card slots across four stain-type zones that shift position each round, creating an ever-changing economy.
🧫
Real Fabric Science
Garment cards reflect genuine textile science — wool requires acidic detergent, silk cannot exceed 30°C, delicate fibres are incompatible with enzyme cleaners. The chemistry is real and learnable through play.
🔬
Four Lab Specialist Roles
Players choose from Chemist, Ecologist, Specialist, or Innovator — each with distinct strategic abilities. Role selection fundamentally shapes how a player engages with the Wash Wheel.
♻️
Sustainability Mechanics
Eco-friendly, low-temperature choices earn sustainability bonus points. High-energy shortcuts are effective but carry long-term penalties — mirroring real environmental trade-offs in the garment care industry.
🤝
Brand Partnership Potential
Natural co-branding potential with laundry brands (Persil, Ariel, Ecover), luxury fashion houses focused on garment longevity, and sustainable textile organisations. The sustainability message aligns with current industry ESG priorities.
🎓
Educational IP
WashWheel covers chemistry, sustainability, and fashion industry science in a form suitable for secondary school, university outreach, and textile industry training programmes.

Garment Cards

Every card reflects real textile science — accurate pH requirements, temperature ranges, enzyme compatibility, and care label data.
