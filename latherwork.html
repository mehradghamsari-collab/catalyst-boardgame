The Fabric Science Game

Players2–4
Duration90–120 min
Age14+
WeightMedium

"Every garment that arrives carries a secret. Read the chemistry correctly — and wash it perfectly."

Latherwork is a strategic card-deck-building eurogame set in a high-end industrial fashion laundry. Build precise wash formulas using real textile chemistry. Compete through the rotating Wash Wheel market. Survive Crisis Events that disrupt everything.

The Origin Story

WHY
LATHERWORK?

The idea began with a carpet. Mehrad was trying to wash a small rug at home and had no idea what the right approach was. He picked up the care label — and found a world he hadn't noticed before. Temperature ranges. Chemistry requirements. Agitation warnings. A whole science encoded in tiny symbols that most people ignore entirely.

"What if people understood this?" he thought. Not just "wash cold" — but why. These aren't arbitrary rules. They're chemistry. And when you ignore them, your clothes suffer — and eventually end up in landfill.

The deeper message of Latherwork is sustainability: when you understand how to care for your garments correctly, they last longer — reducing the overconsumption that makes textile waste one of the world's largest environmental problems.

Unique Market Position

Latherwork is the only strategy game about the science of textile care. It can partner with detergent brands, fashion houses, and sustainability organisations — and belongs in every school teaching chemistry or environmental science.

The Setting

AN INDUSTRIAL
FASHION LAUNDRY.

Your players' boards represent specialist textile care operations in a high-end fashion warehouse — silk evening gowns hanging beside sportswear; steel drums steaming beside chemistry benches. Every garment that arrives is a puzzle requiring the right scientific approach.

The Wash Wheel — the central rotating card market — is the game's beating heart. Resources rotate constantly, creating a shifting economy. Timing the wheel is as important as knowing your fabric chemistry.

The Science

REAL TEXTILE
CHEMISTRY

Every garment has a chemical profile — a fibre composition that determines exactly how it must be treated. Latherwork puts you in the role of the expert who reads those signals and acts on them.

Temperature

Each fibre type responds differently to heat. Reading the dominant fibre — and accounting for minority fibres — determines your safe operating range.

Chemistry

Different fibres require different chemical environments to be cleaned safely. Choosing the wrong approach can cause permanent and irreversible damage.

Agitation & Cycle

Mechanical force that works perfectly on robust fabrics can permanently distort or shrink delicate ones. The right cycle is as critical as the right chemistry.

Every player receives a science reference card. Learning to read it faster than your opponents is the game.

Game Mechanics

HOW TO
PLAY

Each round flows through four phases. The rotating Wash Wheel market creates constant competition — what's available changes each round, and every player's choice affects everyone else's options.

Phase 1
⚠️
CRISIS

An unexpected event disrupts the market or your resources. Crisis cards demand adaptation — flexible strategies survive; rigid ones suffer.

Phase 2
🎡
ACQUISITION

Take one action: acquire from the Wash Wheel, submit a formula, advance your research, or pass. Every choice shapes what remains for everyone else.

Phase 3
🧪
FORMULA

Build and submit your wash formula against a garment card. Scientific precision is rewarded — and errors are penalised.

Phase 4
🔄
END OF ROUND

The market shifts. Resources rotate. When the garment pile runs low, the final scoring round begins.

Central Mechanic

THE WASH
WHEEL MARKET

12 slots. 4 zones. Constantly rotating. The Wash Wheel is the shared resource market — and the engine of competition. Different zones offer different pricing advantages. Cards that no one takes eventually fall off the wheel entirely.

Reading the wheel is as important as reading your fabric chemistry. Timing an acquisition one turn earlier than your opponent — before the wheel rotates past the card you need — is often the margin between scoring and missing out entirely.
