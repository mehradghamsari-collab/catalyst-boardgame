About the Game

A cooperative-competitive eurogame about bioplastics, sustainability, and the price of progress.

Synthelyst places players as CEOs of competing bioplastics companies in 2035 — a near-future world where sustainable materials are both the economic frontier and the last line of ecological defence. Build supply chains, manage clean energy, resolve environmental crises, and outmanoeuvre rivals for market dominance.

Designed by Mehrad Niasari, an active bioplastics R&D researcher at Oxford University, Synthelyst is the only strategy game built on the genuine science and economics of bioplastic production. Every feedstock, every energy system, every polymer trade-off is real.

What makes Synthelyst commercially exceptional is its dual audience: it is a compelling strategy game for the hobby market and a powerful ESG education tool for corporate training, university programmes, and sustainability initiatives.

Key Strengths

What makes
Synthelyst exceptional.

🌿
Real Bioplastics Science
Five real feedstocks — corn, sugarcane, potato, milk protein, algae — each with authentic production profiles, energy requirements, and environmental footprints rooted in actual industrial research.
⚡
Energy Systems
Build solar, wind, hydro, and geothermal energy networks to power production. Fossil fuel options exist — but every shortcut accelerates the shared loss condition.
🌍
Earth Health Mechanic
A shared loss condition that creates genuine cooperative-competitive tension. Players compete for Victory Points while collectively managing planetary collapse. If Earth Health reaches zero, everyone loses — regardless of score.
🏭
Asymmetric Companies
Five distinct company boards with unique strategic identities. Each has a Core ability and a once-per-game Big Bet — creating replayability and genuinely different playstyles.
💼
ESG Education Potential
Synthelyst is actively being considered for corporate ESG training programmes, university sustainability modules, and secondary school science curricula. The educational IP is real and documented.
📦
Prototype Complete
Full prototype ready to play today. Rulebook finalised. Science accuracy verified by a working bioplastics researcher. All design documentation available immediately.

The World Board

Six global production regions, clean energy infrastructure, and the Earth Health tracker — all on one cinematic table presence.
