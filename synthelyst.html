About the Game

A cooperative-competitive strategy game about bioplastics, sustainability, and the price of progress — designed by a working bioplastics researcher.

Synthelyst places players as CEOs of competing bioplastics companies in 2035, a near-future world where sustainable materials are both the economic frontier and the last line of ecological defence.

Designed by Mehrad Niasari, an active bioplastics R&D researcher at Oxford, Synthelyst is unique in the strategy gaming market: a game grounded in genuine science and economics of biomaterial production, with every feedstock, energy system, and polymer trade-off reflecting real industrial practice.

What makes Synthelyst commercially exceptional is its dual audience: a compelling strategy game for the hobby market and a documented education tool for corporate ESG training, university programmes, and sustainability initiatives.
