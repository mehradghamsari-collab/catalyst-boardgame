Players2–5

"Harvest. Build. Polymerise. Save the Earth — or watch it collapse while someone else claims victory."

Synthelyst is a cooperative-competitive eco-strategy game set in 2035. Everyone must keep Earth Health above zero. But only the player with the most VP who has resolved at least one Crisis wins.

The Origin Story

Why
Synthelyst?

Mehrad works in a company focused on producing biodegradable bioplastics from sustainable feedstocks. During fundraising and development he encountered the full complexity of building a green industry — competing companies, regulatory lag, investor pressure, and ecosystems that degrade under over-harvesting.

He realised this wasn't just an industry — it was a game, with real stakes and no easy answers. Anita brought her statistical expertise and deep board game experience to shape the mechanics and ensure every interaction held up under competitive pressure.

Unique Market Position

Synthelyst can be positioned as a sustainability ambassador — showing players in visceral, strategic terms how plastic pollution works and how bioplastics can change it. Ideal for schools and corporate sustainability programmes.

The Stakes

Keep Earth
Health Above 0.

Earth Health starts at 25 and drops with every unresolved Crisis and unchecked pollution. If it hits zero — all players lose immediately. No exceptions.

This is Synthelyst's central tension: you're competing fiercely for VP while depending on rivals to avert global catastrophe. The game models real-world collective action problems with uncomfortable accuracy.

Round Structure

5 Phases.
10 Rounds.

Each round flows through five phases covering the full industrial cycle — from raw material to global consequence. The planet is always watching.

🌾
Work

Gather bioplastic feedstocks from shared ecosystems. Resources are finite — what you take affects what others can access.

⚡
Power

Build and run your energy infrastructure. Every energy choice carries an environmental footprint that compounds across rounds.

♻️
Production

Convert materials into bioplastics and resolve Crises. This is where your strategy pays off — or reveals its gaps.

🌍
Event

A global headline strikes. Some crises can be faced alone. Others demand collective action. Ignoring them costs everyone.

📈
Catalyst

Play Catalyst Cards to build long-term engines and market advantages. Decisions here compound across 10 rounds.
